License & Terms of Use

Plain-English version. The short of it: the art is yours to print and enjoy at home — just don't resell it. Read on for the friendly details.

What you're buying

Every Panini Pano product is a digital download — printable art files (PDF and/or PNG), delivered straight to you the moment your order is done. Each design comes in multiple frame-ready sizes (2:3, 3:4, 4:5 and ISO/A-series) at print-quality 300 DPI, so it fits standard frames from a little 8×10 all the way up to a 24×36 statement piece.

No physical product ships. Nothing arrives in the mail — you download the files and print them yourself, at home, at your local print shop, or through an online printer. That's the whole point: grab it instantly, then take your time making your space beautiful.

What you can do (this is the fun part)

You're buying a personal-use license, which means:

  • Print it as many times as you like — for your own home, a second room, a do-over when the cat knocks the frame off the wall.
  • Print it in any size included in your download.
  • Give a printed copy as a gift — a framed print for a friend, a housewarming, your mum's kitchen.
  • Keep your files and re-download or re-print them whenever you want.

In short: print freely, enjoy freely, for you and the people you love.

What you can't do (the part that keeps artists making art)

This license is for personal use only. Please don't:

  • Resell, share, or give away the digital files themselves — the PDFs/PNGs are for the person who bought them.
  • Sell prints of the art — no print-on-demand, no markets, no shops, no "I'll print and sell these" of any kind.
  • Use the art commercially — no products, packaging, logos, merchandise, or anything you make money from.
  • Claim the art as your own or redistribute it as part of another product, bundle, or freebie.
  • Sub-license, repackage, or upload it anywhere others can download it.

If you'd ever like to use a design commercially, just reach out — we're happy to talk about it.

Who owns the art

Panini Pano (and its artists) keep all copyright and ownership of the designs. Buying a product gives you the right to use the files under this personal-use license — it doesn't transfer ownership of the artwork itself.

Digital goods & refunds

Because every order is a digital download delivered instantly, all sales are final once the files are available to download. We can't "take back" a digital file, so we don't offer refunds on delivered downloads.

That said — we want you to love what you hang on your wall. If something's wrong (a file won't open, you got the wrong item, a download link breaks), email us and we'll make it right, every time.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms now and then as the shop grows. Any changes apply to orders placed after we post them. The version on this page is always the current one.

Questions?

We're real people and we read every message. Email hello@paninipano.com and we'll get back to you.
