Privacy Policy

We're a small art shop, not a data company. Here's exactly what we collect, why, and what we don't do — in plain English.

The short version

We collect the bare minimum needed to send you your art and your receipt. We don't sell your data. We don't store your card details. That's basically it.

What we collect

  • Your email address — so we can deliver your download links and send your receipt. That's the main thing we need.
  • Your order details — which product you bought, so we can give you the right files and help if something goes wrong.

We don't ask for anything we don't need.

Payments

Checkout and payments are handled securely by our payment partner, Whop. Your card details go straight to them — Panini Pano never sees or stores your payment information. Whop processes the transaction under their own privacy and security terms.

Cookies

We keep it light: we use a single cart cookie so your basket remembers what you've added while you shop. That's it — no ad-tracking pixels stuffed into every corner, no selling your browsing habits.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone. Ever.
  • We don't store your payment/card details (that's Whop's job, done securely).
  • We don't send spam. If we ever email you beyond your order and receipts, it'll be because you asked us to — and you can unsubscribe anytime.

Sharing

We only share what's strictly necessary to run the shop — for example, your payment goes through Whop to process your order, and we use a delivery service to send your download. We don't hand your details to anyone for marketing.

Your data, your call

It's your information. If you'd like us to delete your data, or you want to know what we have on file, just email us and we'll take care of it.

Contact

Privacy questions, deletion requests, or anything else — email hello@paninipano.com. A real person will reply.
