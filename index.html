Frequently Asked Questions

Wall art

How fast do I get my files?

Instantly. Right after checkout you get a download link to a ZIP of high-res files — no shipping, no waiting.

What format and resolution are the files?

High-res JPG and PDF at 300 DPI, sized to the largest frame in each ratio family. Every print is delivered in four ratios: 2:3, 3:4, 4:5, and ISO/A-series.

What sizes can I print? Will they fit my frame?

Yes. The four included ratios cover the most common frame sizes: 8×10, 11×14, 16×20, 18×24, 24×36, plus A4 and A3. See the Sizes & frames section above for the full list.

Where can I print them?

Anywhere. Home printer (matte cardstock 200gsm+ looks great), local print shop (Staples, FedEx Office), or an online printer (Printful, Walmart Photo, Mpix, Posterjack). For best results print at the listed sizes or below.

What can I use the prints for? Personal or commercial?

Personal use license — print as many copies as you like for your own home, gifts, or single-room decor. Commercial / resale is not included. (Full license text in the footer.)

What's your refund policy?

Because every order is an instant digital download, sales are final once files are delivered. If something is wrong with the files or you can't access your download, email us and we'll fix it.

Coloring books

What makes Panini Pano coloring books different?

Every page is hand-designed for adult-level detail — intricate line work, varied themes (nature, mandalas, optical illusions, humor), and premium single-sided paper. Each book has 18–28 unique designs with no repeats.

Paperback vs. digital PDF collections — what's the difference?

Our 9 paperback books are printed on premium single-sided paper and ship via Amazon. The 10 digital PDF collections (launching) are instant-download files you print at home — color as many copies as you like, forever.

Can I use markers or gel pens?

Yes. Paperbacks use single-sided printing so markers won't bleed through to the next design. For digital PDFs, we recommend 120gsm+ cardstock for markers and watercolors.

When will the digital PDF collections be available?

Soon. Click "Notify Me" on any digital collection above and we'll email you the moment it's ready. Early subscribers get a launch discount.
